# TRES QUARTOS — Brand Reference

Compraventa de artículos deportivos. Especialidad: **rugby**. Expansión: todos los deportes.

---

## Identity

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tagline** | "Donde el rugby sigue vivo." |
| **Name origin** | Three-quarter: la línea de backs que conecta juego, velocidad e inteligencia |
| **Positioning** | Rugbiers hablando con rugbiers. El marketplace que conoce el deporte. |

**Mission:** Hacer el deporte accesible facilitando la compraventa de equipamiento de calidad.

**Vision:** Ser el referente de compraventa de rugby en Argentina, expandiéndose a todos los deportes.

**Values:** Comunidad · Honestidad · Accesibilidad · Pasión · Confianza

## Visual Identity

### Colors

| Name | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Amarillo TQ | `#F7C200` | Primary, accents, CTA, logo letters |
| Carbón TQ | `#424242` | Logo inner circle, backgrounds |
| Negro TQ | `#000000` | Logo outer background, max contrast |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Clean backgrounds |
| Gray | `#6B6B6B` | Secondary text |

**Color ratio:** 60% base (carbón/white) · 30% neutral · 10% Amarillo TQ

### Typography

| Role | Font | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Titles, taglines, prices | Barlow Condensed | Bold / SemiBold |
| Body, descriptions | Inter | Regular / Light |

Both fonts free on Google Fonts.

### Logo Construction (accurate from PNG)

The logo has 3 layers: black outer background → yellow gold ring → charcoal inner circle.

**T:** Geometric with Art Deco terminals — small inward hooks at crossbar ends, inline (double-line) vertical stroke.

**Q:** Built as a **sports racket** — concentric circles as the racket face, grip handle extending bottom-right with parallel lines. This is the key brand differentiator.

**"TRES QUARTOS":** Curved along the top inner circle, uppercase, wide letter-spacing (tracking).

### Logo Rules

- Always maintain clear space equal to the height of the Q on all sides
- Min size: 40px digital / 15mm print
- Variants: full color · inverted (white) · monochrome · icon only (TQ sigil)
- Never: stretch, rotate, recolor, add effects, reduce clear space
- The Q-as-racket must always be preserved — it's the core brand symbol

## Voice & Tone

**Personality:** Direct, passionate, peer-to-peer. Not corporate.

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Product post | Dry, honest, rugby terms | `"Botines Canterbury scrum T.42 — tapones completos. $22.000"` |
| Stories | Energetic, short, attitude | `"¿Tenés botines guardados? Alguien los necesita para el próximo partido."` |
| DM reply | Friendly, efficient | `"Hola! Sí están. ¿Te mando más fotos?"` |
| Value content | Didactic, no pretense | `"Cómo elegir tapones según la cancha 👇"` |

**Key phrases:**
- "Donde el rugby sigue vivo."
- "Jugado. Vendido. Vuelto a jugar."
- "Del vestuario a tu equipo."
- "Equipate bien. Pagá menos."
- "El equipamiento que ya dio todo tiene más para dar."

**Never say:** corporate speak, generic sports language, marketing fluff.

## Photography

**Product:** White/neutral background · natural light · min 1080x1080px
**Always show:** brand label, size, real wear condition, sole/stitching detail
**Lifestyle:** Real players, real fields. Scrums, lineouts, training, mud. No stock photos.
**Editing:** Slight contrast boost · natural saturation · warm-neutral temp · no heavy filters

---

## Graphic Elements

- **Lines:** Thin horizontal lines in Amarillo TQ — motion, speed
- **Circle:** Heritage from isotipo — use as frame or focal point
- **Texture:** Rugby field grid or ball leather pattern at low opacity
- **Icons:** Outline style, 2px stroke, never solid fill
